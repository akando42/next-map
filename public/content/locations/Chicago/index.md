### The Great Lake and American Mississippi River Heartland

The Great Lake of North America is the largest fresh water lake in the world. This lake was created during the Ice Age when the North Pole Icy Plate movements between seasons slowly eroding the ground surface area around the modern day border between Canada and United States. 

During winter, the Great Lake become a Frozen Ice Surface that is thick enough for humans and vehicles to travel on top of it. During summer, melting snow and ice from the Great Lake would flood the Mississippi River with excessive amount of water that flows across America from the Northern Great Lake region to the Gulf of Mexico in the South. 

The Great Lake and Mississippi snow and rain seasonal water flow creates highly fertile landscape that enables development of large human and animal population here before the arrival of Western European colonial power.  Very few Tibetan East Asian made it to the Mississippi river delta through the Siberian-Alaskan ice bridge during the Ice Age ended up developing into a large population of Native American tribes along the Mississippi River delta. 

Native American tribes like the Comanche near modern day Dallas raised large fleets of Idaho Mustang horses as their main of mode of hunting and transportation across the flat Mongolian-Steppe like climate of North America central region. 

These Native-American horse archers initially posted serious threats to the newly found colonies of French and British navy along the Atlantics East Coast of North America. Continuous attacks from hostile Native American horse archers tribes stopped most of the British and French sailors and volunteer explorers to build farms and cities beyond the West Virginia mountain range. 

Without large-scale farming or horses, the population counts of new arrivals from Western European nations stayed relative low with supplies still need to be transported across the Atlantics to East Coast port cities. The serious decline of Native American population only started with the large scale farming in the South Eastern states of Florida and Alabama. Slaves arriving from West African ports enabled Western European plantation owners to develop large fleet of castles like Sheep, Cows and Horses to enable a mobile expansion on land into the Mississippi Central Region. 

Western European cowboy gunmen with Native American Idaho Mustang horses would overrun Native American bow and arrow tribes. The Tomahawk axe throwing Native Tribes who were once the main cause of population decline for Western European colonies now become target practices for the Cowboy horse riding sharp shooters from Alabama and Florida. 

After the Cowboys mass extermination of Native American population in the Mississippi river delta, the Western European and African settlements in North America became self-sustainable via large scale Wheat farming in region around the Great Lake near modern day Chicago and Indianapolis. 

Nowadays, only a small number of Native American tribes left in United States and Canada with most of the surviving Native American being kept within the Native American conservation area in the state of Idaho and Nevada.  The American border expanded beyond the West Virginia mountain range all the way to the Pacific West Coast along the travel path of first American horse-riding Cowboys then American railroad workers and finally American Ford Mustang gasoline power cars. 

Modern American gene pool now contains mostly of new immigrants from Asia, Europe and Africa with African Americans being much more athletic than Native Africans, Asian Americans being much more intelligent than Native Asians and European Americans being much more attractive than Native Europeans.
