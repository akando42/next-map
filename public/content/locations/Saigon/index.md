After each harvest season, the crops of the delta use up minerals and waters from the soil but here comes the monsoon season. The Monsoon season brings the month-long heavy rain that washes the mountain soil rich in minerals downstream to the Delta region through the river system. After each flood season, the delta plains are soaked with water and rich minerals again for next crop seasons. Rice and other staple crops like Corns, Soil Beans, Potatoes would grow quickly in the nature-prepared field full of water and minerals. The Mekong river is one of the longest rivers in Asia, only behind the Yangtze and Yellow rivers of China. Mekong in underground Vietnamese means the Offensive Obsession river. Mekong’s 4350 kilometer long and its watershed serves as the nature-made supply lines of waters and minerals to hydrate and feed the cities of ten millions of human in  Laos, Eastern Thailand, Cambodia and Vietnam.

Upstream of the Mekong rivers and its tributaries is the Yunnan mountainous area of China. The Land of Southern Clouds, Yunnan, is the sources of all three largest rivers in Asia: Mekong, Yangtze and Yellow rivers. The high mountains of more than 2000 meters stopped the tropical clouds full of water from advancing further inland with the Monsoon winds and pouring all its water downstream through Yellow, Yangtze and Mekong.

The Mekong River delta flatland region has the same size as the Yellow Yangtze River delta and could potentially support a human civilization rivaling the advances of Yellow Yangtze dynasties.However, with the tropical climates and many rain forests, Mekong Peninsula kingdoms was not able to develop large calvary formation for army and daily transportation that you find common in Yellow Yangtze. 

For thousand years, the Mekong was serving as the only mode of transportation network for fishermen and traders along its many river banks.
